# EaSee Kids • Joyful Dyslexia Reading & Motor Adventures 🌟

An evidence-based, interactive web application crafted with senior art direction to help dyslexic children (ages 4 and up) identify, practice, and overcome confusing letter and number reversals (e.g. **b vs d**, **5 vs S**, **p vs q**, **2 vs Z**, **m vs w**, **u vs n**, **6 vs 9**, **E vs 3**).

---

## 🎨 Pedagogical & Clinical Features

1. **Daily 5 to 10-Minute Visual & Physical Exercise Routine:**
   - **Step 1: Multisensory Mnemonic Briefing**: Understand the character's secret identity (e.g. *Barnaby Bear's big belly walking forward* vs *Daisy Duck's tail in the back*; *Bat first then Ball* vs *Drum then Stick*).
   - **Step 2: Visual Discrimination Hunter (Balloon Pop)**: Fast-paced target identification amidst mirror distractors, calibrated to the child's age.
   - **Step 3: Directional Muscle Memory Tracing (Rainbow Canvas)**: Finger/mouse drawing with numbered checkpoints reinforcing proper stroke order.
   - **Step 4: Bilateral Midline & Visual Gym**: 
     - **Lazy 8 Infinity Loop**: Eye-tracking exercise crossing the visual midline to synchronize brain hemispheres.
     - **Cross-Crawl Rhythm Tap**: Stand up and touch opposite hand to knee to stimulate bilateral brain integration.
     - **The "b-e-d" Bed Trick**: Physical hand orientation mnemonic using both thumbs.
   - **Step 5: Joyous Celebration**: Confetti bursts, star coins, and streak maintenance!

2. **Endless Practice Lab vs. 30-Day Guided Quest:**
   - **30-Day Guided Adventure Map**: 30 unlockable island stages across 4 themed worlds (*Whispering Woods*, *Crystal Cove*, *Sky Peaks*, *Star Castle & Graduation*).
   - **Endless Arcade**: Unlimited play across 5 mini-games for any confusion pair at any time.

3. **Dyslexia-Centric Sensory Accessibility Studio:**
   - **Dyslexic Font Toggle**: Switch between `Lexend Deca` (designed for fluent reading spacing) and `OpenDyslexic` (gravity-weighted bottom letterforms).
   - **Anti-Glare Paper Tints**: Clinically tested background tints (*Warm Ivory*, *Soft Peach*, *Mint Dew*, *Lavender Breeze*, *Sky Blue*) to eliminate Scotopic sensitivity and "letter vibration".
   - **Guided Reading Ruler**: Interactive translucent highlighter guide following mouse/touch to prevent line jumping.
   - **Web Speech & Web Audio Narration**: Built-in sound effects (marimbas, bubble pops, fanfares, rhythm metronomes) with zero external audio assets.

4. **Zero-Friction Onboarding & Cross-Device Sync:**
   - **No Mandatory Registration**: Immediately starts playing with child's age, gender/avatar, and stores 100% of progress locally in `localStorage`.
   - **Cross-Device Pickup**: Generate a friendly 6-digit sync code (e.g. `STAR-77`), optional email link delivery, or offline JSON backup to switch between Laptop, Desktop, iPad/Tablet, or Mobile Phone.

5. **Parent & Educator Diagnostic Dashboard:**
   - Precision accuracy percentages per confusion pair.
   - Identifies strengths vs. areas needing focus.
   - **Printable Super Reader Champion Achievement Certificate** ready for classroom or home display.
